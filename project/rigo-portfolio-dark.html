About

My career has followed the data from the warehouse to the boardroom. Retail inventory, SAP operations, S&OP, global pipeline governance: the job has stayed the same. Connect what sales expects, what operations can supply and what finance plans, then make the data clean enough that leaders trust it.

Step 1

Start from the decision a leader needs to make, not the dataset.

Step 2

Fix data quality and governance first, so every report after it holds up.

Step 3

Automate the repeatable parts, then add AI where it saves real time.

Step 4

Close the loop by tracking actions and forecast accuracy over time.
